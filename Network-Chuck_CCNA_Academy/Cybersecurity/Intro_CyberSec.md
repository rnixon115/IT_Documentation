## Current State of Cybersecurity

- Cyber threats have changed dynamically since the 1970's. When the World Wide Web (WWW) launched in the 90's, malware grew exponentially across the internet.  File-sharing platforms such as Napster, Kazaa, and others spread infections. To counter malware, McAfee, Norton, and others emerged to protect the public.
- Modern threats have sophisticated processes like ransomware, persistent threats, and AI-driven attacks. Cybercriminals and nation-state agencies exploit cloud weaknesses, legacy systems (Deprecated Software/Hardware), and IoT devices.  Artificial Intelligence enhances cyber threats by automating phishing (Malicious Emails), deepfakes, and stealthy reconnaissance.
- Cybercriminals utilize advanced software applications to bypass detection, target critical infrastructure, and steal confidential data, resulting in financial hardship, legal .
