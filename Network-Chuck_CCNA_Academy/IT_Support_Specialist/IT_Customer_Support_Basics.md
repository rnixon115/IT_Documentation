## Ticket Flow & Queue Management
1. Help Desk agents are the central point of contact when an issue occurs. The communication type can be email, voice, chat, or in-person.
2. So, the goal is to provide services to end-users who are having issues with their technical problems.
3. So, ticket flow is determined based on urgency and SLA for the customer.
4. When an issue is too complicated or will take much longer, the agent will escalate the problem with final notes on what they tried.
5. Queue Management is about keeping end-user service satisfaction high and the smoothness of operations.
6. Queue management is also about labeling tickets by severity. Password resets are low, network outages are medium, and a file server that is down is severe.

## Documentation & Professionalism 
1. Summarize the Issue Clearly
2. Detail the troubleshooting steps taken
3. Highlight the resolution or next steps
4. Use Professional & Neutral Language
5. Ensure Confidentiality
- #### Best Documentation Practices
  1. Start the report with a clear problem statement. "End-user reports that their Windows PC can't connect to the Wi-Fi"
  2. List the steps taken in troubleshooting the issue. List the steps in an ordered list, from beginning to the end. 
  3. Include relevant details: Include any updates, tools used, or commands executed to tell future troubleshooters what changes you made.
  4. Note the outcome of each step: Noting the outcome of each step clarifies what action assisted in fixing the issue.
  5. Highlight the Successes & Failures: The successes and failures of each action.
  6. Document any End-user actions: Document if the end-user performs actions that can hinder or assist in future troubleshooting.
  7. Resolution: Clearly state the solution to the issue. If not, then outline the recommended steps to take or the escalation procedure.
## Problem Solving
1. Define the problem.
2. Gather detailed information.
3. Identify a probable cause of the failure.
4. Devise a plan to resolve the problem.
5. Make necessary changes to implement the plan.
6. Observe the results of the changes.
7. If the problem is not resolved, repeat the process.
8. Document the changes made to resolve the problem.
