
## Display Troubleshooting Quick Guide:
- #### Pixel
  1. Pixels are filled with red, green, and blue (RGB) dots that make up the display and image quality. The more pixels filling the screen, the better the picture quality.
- #### Dot Pitch
  1. When grainy text appears or pixels are far apart on the screen, it's most likely an issue with DOTS or the distance between pixels.
- #### Brightness
  1. Brightness sets how illuminated the screen is.
- #### Contrast Ratio
  1. This is a measurement of how white and how black a monitor can get. A contrast ratio of 1,000:1 displays dimmer whites and more pale blacks than 4,500:1. To help the customer adjust the contrast, search for "contrast", select Contrast themes, and have them turn on a contrast theme by selecting one from the dropdown menu. Options include Aquatic, Desert, Dusk, and Night sky. These themes create high-contrast displays that can make text and interface elements more visible for certain users.
- #### Aspect Ratio
  1. Aspect ratio what defines the ratio images on the screen. If the resolution is set to a lower dimension than what is recommended. It can make the screen can look too narrow or too wide.
- #### Refresh Rate
  1. Refresh rate is measure in Hertz (Hz) and how fast the monitor can redraw the screen. For example, playing a video game requires a higher refresh rate due to the amount of changes on the screen.
- #### Other Troubleshooting Tips.
  1. Check the connections and make sure that the cables work. Always keep a spare just in case.
  2. When display is flickering, make sure that the refresh rate is set to the optimal choice or change the inverter inside the monitor.
  3. When the display shows horzonital lines or a glitchy screen. Check the underlying hardware of the monitor or computer to find a disconnected cable. If the cable is connected, make sure that there is no dust or debris inhibiting the GPU cooling systems. If there is no dust or obstacles, GPU may be faulty and time for a new one.
  4. If you're seeing a ghost display, "display is experiencing burn-in". Make sure to unlpug the power for a few hour. If the problem persist, then try degaussing the screen if applicable. If not, then replace the monitor.
  5. Images on the screen look distorted, then the driver has been corrupted or the display settings are misconfigure. Update or reinstall the driver in Safe mode or correct the settings.
  6. Overly sized icons and or images can be resolve by changing the settings or replacing the driver in safe mode.
