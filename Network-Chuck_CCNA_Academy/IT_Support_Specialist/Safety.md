## Basic Safety Procedure 

### Electrical Safety

- Always read the OEM manual to prevent fires, injuries, and fatalities.
- Power supplies and other components can contain high voltage. Even after the device is turned off, it may still retain a dangerous level of voltage.
- Electrical devices have specific power requirements that must be met. AC adapters and power supplies are designed for a particular power output. If the power input or output exceeds the designated levels, it can damage the adapters or the electronic devices themselves.
- Electrical equipment must be properly grounded at all times to ensure safety and prevent harm.

#### Electrostatic Discharge (ESD)

- Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) can occur when static electricity builds up on one surface and then comes into contact with another surface that has a different electric charge.
- ESD can cause damage to sensitive electronic components, so it is important to take precautions to prevent it.
