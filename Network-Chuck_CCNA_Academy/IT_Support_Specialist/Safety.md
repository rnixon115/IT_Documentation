## Basic Safety Procedure 

#### Electrical Safety

- Make sure to read the OEM manual to prevent fires, injuries, and fatalities.
- Power supplies and other components contain high voltage. Even after you turn it off, the device can still retain high voltage.
- Electrical devices have power requirements that must be met. AC adapters and power supplies are manufactured to a specific power output. If the power output or input is too high or too low, it can damage the power adapters or the electronic devices.
- Electrical equipment must be grounded at all times to prevent harm

##### ESD 

- Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) can occur when there is a buildup of static electricity from a surface coming into contact with another differently charged surface.
